A. What the selected effect requires

For valid fixed source/context/proof/PI and an accepting completed D, the chosen
successful effect requires that the same declared r remain eligible and that the
same scoped (a,n) remain unused. Its successful write changes exactly that key
FALSE to TRUE, preserving all other keys/history/product state relative to the
immediate pre-commitment state. It has no liveness or arbitrary source-adoption
promise. Both gates and final indivisibility remain independently required.

ROOT-ELIGIBLE and SCOPED-USED are individually necessary: a case with unused key
but expired r must not commit; a case with eligible r but already consumed key
must not commit. One bit cannot represent both independently owned source facts.
They are collectively sufficient only within the fully validated source/context,
completed E/D, source-applicability and atomic-frame domain. Outside that domain
there is no valid two-bit equivalence decision or claimed selected capability.

B. Materiality and validation are separate

For complete valid v,b, EQ=v AND NOT b and MC=NOT v OR b are disjoint/exhaustive.
A qualified v=FALSE or b=TRUE independently supplies MC after earlier validation.
Inability to validate a fact is not a material fact about its value. Partial E
cannot produce D even with an independently known negative. Both-true and
complete-but-uncovered guards, all 21 recovery rows and old failure precedence
remain mandatory.

Identity/source/authority/profile mismatch; malformed or incoherent PI/artifacts;
unqualified verifier result; unknown evidence/visibility; incomplete policy
applicability or invalid source status are validation/admission failures. They
cannot become a third projected bit, be called root expiry, or be ignored because
v,b happen to match. The current source contract assigns no further material
predicate. This is a bounded source reading, not a universal claim for arbitrary
future security policies or implementations.

C. Fixed coordinate coverage and minimality

SEMANTIC-BASIS fixes specification, relevant type/rule/domain/root/nullifier/proof/
artifact source meanings, scopes and exact PI. E-BASIS separately fixes A/E/C/V/Z,
proof/artifact binding and historical cuts. Mixing them would conflate fixed
semantics with historical observation evidence. Current gate cuts remain current
evidence; equality of those cuts is neither required nor enough.

The existing authorization header already binds exact mapping/profile bytes and
digests. S90 security policy, algorithms, assumptions, experiments and assessor
qualification contracts are bound by the immutable final PRIVACY/profile digest.
They do not need redundant additional authorization coordinates. A policy change
changes the profile digest and invalidates the old join; it is not an equivalent
change to a projected BOOL3. The security contract must not be changed under an
existing profile identifier/digest merely by signing new evidence.

S90 adds actual policy-applicability/source/assessor reads to the evidence closure
where consumed. Their authority, bytes, context and runtime applicability must
be independently established. They are not discarded because they are outside
the pure expression AST or because profile bytes are immutable. Exact reference
metadata is not proof of scope adequacy.

E. Applicability of computational policy and non-vacuity

A governing policy must establish the full adversary/observer/envelope/assumption
scope and every required experiment, including repeated/adaptive whole-view
behavior. It cannot exclude inherited actor targets, erase a known observation
or declare an established linkage acceptable. Collision resistance does not
supply tracking or actor-information security. A current applicability claim must
be source-qualified for its actual context; a Boolean TRUE input or unchanged
policy digest cannot establish it.

No exact Poseidon parameter set, coherent proof-artifact set, bound policy or
qualified assessor is established in this packet. Therefore no real selected
profile's adequacy, non-vacuous security or admission is asserted. This is an
explicit external instantiation/evidence boundary. The reviewer must assess
whether the proposed parameterized definition is sufficient without those actual
appointments, and whether delegation of bounds/experiment qualification to the
governing source leaves a protocol-policy hole. A signature or merely calling a
source competent cannot close that hole.

The two projection rows identify selected dependencies; they are not full Dep(D).
The full closure includes every executed read, status, argument, control and

S90 ASSESSOR-EVENT and POLICY-APPLICABILITY schemas supply the new evidence

Unchanged-D sufficiency requires exact complete replay and a full fresh map of
actual dependencies. A changed history may be gate-immaterial yet defeat recovery
sufficiency. Unchanged v,b, the fixed coordinate pair, static policy digest, or a
successful UNION calculation is insufficient. Source inability yields unresolved
