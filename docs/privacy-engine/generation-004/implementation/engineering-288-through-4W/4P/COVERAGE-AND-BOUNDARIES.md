## Executable construction and validation

`ledger_kernel.construct` acquires exact material through the existing SOURCE-REF owner/source/anchor/material checks and static dependency checker, then constructs canonical accepted OBLIGATION and COMPOSITION templates. Missing bytes, changed digests, unsupported grammar, invalid model references, cycles, wrong policy scope and missing classifier authority fail. No result-dependent/static digest cycle is introduced.

The concrete source acquisition grammar `CP4D-FINITE-LEDGER-SOURCE-V1` is **laboratory mechanism metadata**, not a protocol schema change or a source appointment. It contains exact template classifications, mandatory exact-duty assignments, full-graph coverage recipes, source-established decomposition terms/repetition units/ranges and exact NV input binding. These source facts are authenticated only within the declared finite LAB provider; its independent semantic competence/whole-view correspondence remains an explicit hypothesis. No assertion bit alone produces a pass.

The checker interprets coverage intervals over exact model node indexes, structural occurrence ordinals and potential-left stripes. A stripe includes **every** legal right endpoint under the retained predicate, with no supplied filter. Legal bounds, canonical intervals and normalized union establish membership/uniqueness before cardinality is compared. Multiple claims covering the same stripe do not compensate for an omitted stripe. Every exact family must cover its entire finite node/structural review scope. Every computational experiment must independently cover the entire finite graph, including structural interactions; another experiment's coverage cannot fill a hole. This is the scope of this concrete finite mechanism, not a newly imposed universal ledger encoding. A different source mechanism would need its own qualified parser/coverage proof.

Node/edge reference correctness alone is insufficient. Classification templates must match independently acquired source construction; external records must match the templates and final model/profile identities. An exact duty cannot be assigned to a computational waiver. Missing/orphan/duplicate/conflicting duties, incomplete targets/views and missing source bytes reject. The known observation checker remains responsible for established source-qualified violations; budgets cannot clear them.

Composition checks cover exactly one rule per required experiment, all applicable distinct arguments, aligned positive multiplicities, complete obligation coverage, exact experiment/envelope hashes and source-declared repetition units/ranges. Repetition ranges are checked against the corresponding source-owned occurrence/comparison/attempt limit. Unknown units or missing decomposition evidence are inability; known over-limit scope is INVALID-ACTION-CONTEXT. Qualified UNION arithmetic uses exact fractions and caps at one. A tighter theorem remains independently qualified; unsupported theorems cannot establish a budget or disposition. Unknown/invalid terms never become zero.

NV checks now run for **every required experiment even when its ARGUMENT is absent**. NV input identity covers policy/world references, envelope, leakage, full model, target/coalition/label directories and qualification contracts. NV1's reduced strict maximum remains enforced by the accepted grammar. The finite checker tests genuine alternatives, matched leakage/schedule, nonzero support and normalized complete finite distributions; uniform full-source-class/view adequacy remains conditional on the explicit external LAB qualifications. Unsupported/unavailable NV cannot establish a bound. There is no assertion that this finite checker proves concrete cryptographic security.

## Exact retained-model/record-grammar obstacle

`results/record-grammar-boundary.json` and `reproduce_record_boundary.py` reproduce this incompatibility:

* Retained node 36511 is `SYMBOLIC-U-ONE::SECURITY:ALGORITHM.$record::CP2-ALGORITHM-000001`, with literal surface `SECURITY:ALGORITHM.$record`.
* The selected finite contract explicitly includes that surface in its complete `field_label_surfaces` directory.
* Accepted S90 `v4_record_grammar.py` defines `ID` as `[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9._:#-]*`. OBLIGATION.node_ids is LIST-ID; FIELD-LABEL.surface_id is ID. Both accepted grammar checks reject the literal `$` identity. The implementation reproduces the rejection.
* The accepted exact-model-reference clauses and retained model/contract identities cannot be satisfied by silently omitting those classes or rewriting the identifier here. The 4B wire-token relaxation is not a change to these S90 record grammars.

This is a **specific compatibility obstacle between the retained model/selected contract and accepted record grammar**, not a broad reopened definition review or an asserted universal impossibility of S90. Even supplying the missing qualifications would not, by itself, make these literal records pass the preserved grammar. No escape convention, alias, broader primitive grammar, renamed token, reduced model or revised definition was invented. A narrowly authoritative resolution is needed before literal full-model FIELD-LABEL/OBLIGATION instantiation. All independent construction/validation work above is complete despite that obstacle.
