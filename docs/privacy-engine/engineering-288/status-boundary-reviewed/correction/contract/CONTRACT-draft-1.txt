**Proposed owning contract: `ManagedAuthority.status` — prospective managed integration, draft 1.**

**Recommendation:** keep `status()` as an internal connection-management and diagnostic interface, with no public status endpoint or automatic per-call persistence. Retain exact occurrences when they are deliberately captured or consumed as evidence. This is a proposed integration restriction and evidence contract—not a claim that the existing code enforces it or that transient calls have no privacy significance.

The contract is decision-ready for its integration choices. Security qualification and complete model instantiation remain blocked.

**1. Existing behaviour and boundary**

The controlling implementation is `compiler/live_connection.py`, SHA-256:

`7344170ca01a2dbbd3afbeb4a6a55c0b95db0edb1a8d74e4fa4638b6dcfc6da5`

Source-established facts:

- `status()` reads the managed object under its `RLock` and returns a new dictionary. It performs no database access, journal insertion or allowance charge (`:23–24`).
- It does not require `READY`; connection status remains readable in closed or quarantined states.
- `recover()` embeds one normal status result after successful validation and publication of `READY` (`:90–92`).
- `open_session()` calls `recover()` and discards its return before returning the managed object and scheduler (`:101–104`).
- The declared acceptance boundary is one managed owner, trusted process and serialized access. Protection against introspection, competing owners and rollback is expressly excluded from the implementation claim.
- The retained observation consumer supports status-call correspondence, but is not deployment attestation.

The source does not establish a network endpoint, complete caller inventory or access-control enforcement for this method.

**3. Exposure outside the trusted process**

The proposed integration provides **no direct service exposure** of the status result outside the trusted process. That includes forwarding the nested `recover().status` through a public response.

Status-bearing evidence may leave the process through a separately authorized evidence/export workflow. Such an export is an actual disclosure and copy event. Its recipients, storage, lineage and relevant observer relationships must be covered; the word “evidence” supplies no privacy exemption.

This preserves the already retained handoff and close records. It neither retracts those disclosures nor declares their custody qualified.

“No public endpoint selected” is not proof of no external observation. Process operators, debuggers, logs, crash material, storage and scheduling channels remain subject to the governing observer model.

**5. Transient use and retained evidence**

**Transient use:** an internal call may return information for immediate management or diagnosis without creating a separate durable status receipt. The integration adds no automatic per-call logger.

This does not waive another governing retention duty. If a particular use requires exact evidence that the transient path cannot supply, that use cannot claim evidential completeness.

**Retained use:** a result becomes retained evidence when a governed capture/export records it, or when it is relied upon to establish a claimed occurrence or recovery correspondence. The following rules then apply:

- Preserve the original captured artifact bytes, including required surrounding invocation, parent, state and exception material.
- Identify the capture/acquisition, its exact artifact digest, source identity, applicable context, managed-instance binding and location of the status occurrence.
- Within an existing managed trace, use its frame, parent and sequence identities. These are capture-local; they must not masquerade as global call identities.
- Bind an embedded recovery status to its actual child status frame when that trace evidence exists.
- If a standalone saved status object lacks call-frame or instance evidence, retain it as that bounded saved assertion. Do not manufacture the missing invocation history.

A Python return dictionary has no intrinsic serialized byte representation. Where the existing managed recorder captures it, retain that recorder’s exact artifact bytes and value representation. Existing JSON handoff/close records retain their original bytes. This contract does **not** introduce a new canonical status serializer or silently re-encode old evidence.

A later export is a distinct retained copy/acquisition. It must preserve origin lineage and must not be counted as another status invocation. Equal content alone neither proves shared origin nor authorizes merging copies.

**6. Correspondence to the existing model**

The proposed correspondence uses the existing separation required by S90:

- **Static coverage:** status fields, owning return, framing, recovery embedding, managed state, lock/control flow, exceptions and applicable observer relationships belong to the source-derived declarations in both symbolic contexts. Multiplicities and guards require established scope; the first captured trace is not that scope.
- **Actual occurrences:** separate calls remain separate owning occurrences within their authenticated or otherwise supported capture lineage. Their values, parent joins and dispositions follow the actual evidence.
- **Copies:** separately retained copies preserve occurrence provenance and add applicable copy/observer relationships without becoming additional invocations.
- **Unavailable evidence:** missing instance, caller, return, timing or lineage facts remain explicit gaps. They cannot become zeros, absent events or qualified classifications.

The retained validator already checks exact field membership, literal constants, phase/epoch correspondence and the nested recovery-status relationship (`compiler/managed_observations.py:349–369`). Its check of `reason` establishes string type, not complete provenance of that text. This contract does not upgrade that check.

No new token layout, multiplicity codec, global call identifier or relationship exception is proposed. Final ranges and qualified symbolic coverage remain unresolved under S90’s static/actual universe rules, lines **445–483**, and byte adapter, lines **748–781**.
