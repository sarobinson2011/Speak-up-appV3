ownership coordinates use their declared unique keys. Responses count only non-null

their count agrees with permanent attempt counters. Backup identifiers use U and

history occurrences. The source-initial empty U contributes zero pending instances;
the latest head is not counted as an extra history beyond its event. The single
export acquisition is not multiplied by repeated archive-member representations.

Public scope is the existing exact acquisition and both context references. This

empty supplied scope lists have arithmetic zero only within that conditional list;
